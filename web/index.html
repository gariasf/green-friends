Plant care app for iPhone

What your houseplants need, and when.

Add your plants, and Green Friends keeps track of when each one needs water, fertilizer or a bigger pot. Every day it shows you the few that need you, and nothing else.

Free · iPhone with iOS 26 or later

  • 458 houseplant species, each with its own schedule and Care Guide
  • 1 notification a day, and only when a plant needs you
  • 0 accounts, ads or trackers

Schedules

Every plant on its own schedule

Choose the species from a catalog of 458 houseplants, and its watering, fertilizing and repotting intervals come with it. Change any of them for one plant, or give a plant a schedule all its own.

  • Watering and fertilizing follow the seasons. You set the growing months, and care slows down or pauses for the rest of the year.
  • Toxic or non-toxic to pets, wherever a published reference settles it.

Care Guides

Know how, not just when

Every species has a Care Guide: how to water, feed, place and pot it in the growing season and the dormant one, with a fun fact at the end.

  • Brown tips, webbing, yellow leaves? Symptoms list the likely causes, how to tell them apart, and what to do.

Care Log

Remember what you did, and when

Log a watering, a feed, a repot or a note, today or on an earlier day, and it goes in that plant's Care Log, beside its photo.

  • Soil still damp? Tap Not dry yet, and watering comes round again a little later.

Your Garden stays on your iPhone

There's nothing to sign up for. Your plants, photos and notes live on your phone, and come along in your iPhone backup. Export the whole Garden as one file whenever you like, and Import it on another iPhone.

Questions

Anything else? Write to greenfriends@gariasf.com.

Is Green Friends free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and nothing to unlock.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, with iOS 26 or later. There's no iPad, Mac or Android version.

How does it know when to water?

Each species comes with watering, fertilizing and repotting intervals for the growing and the dormant season. Green Friends counts from the last time you logged that care, and you can change any interval for one plant.

What if my plant isn't in the catalog?

Add it anyway: give it a name and a care schedule of its own.

Will it tell me if a plant is toxic to my cat or dog?

Where a published reference such as the ASPCA's plant lists settles it, a plant shows Toxic to pets or Non-toxic to pets. Where none does, it says nothing. It's no substitute for a vet: if a pet has eaten a plant, call your vet or an animal poison line.

How do I move my plants to a new iPhone?

Restore your iPhone backup and your Garden comes with it. You can also Export on the old iPhone and Import on the new one.

Does it work in the Southern Hemisphere?

The growing season starts out as March to October, and you can set your own months in Settings. For now, the App Store offers Green Friends in northern-hemisphere countries.
