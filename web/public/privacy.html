Green Friends

Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026. It covers Green Friends as the App Store offers it.

In short

Green Friends collects no data. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app sends nothing about you or your Garden anywhere. What you add stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your plants, their Care Logs and notes, their photos and your settings live in the app's own storage on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone, to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes them, and Apple's privacy policy covers it.

Photos

You add a plant's photo with the camera or from your library. The app uses the camera only to take that photo. A photo from your library comes through iOS's own picker, so the app sees only the photo you pick, never the rest of your library. It keeps a smaller copy of the photo, without its location, and nothing else.

The Daily Digest

The Daily Digest is a notification your iPhone schedules for itself, planned on the phone from your own Garden. Nothing is sent to plan it, and you can turn it off in your iPhone's Settings.

Exports

Export garden makes one file with your plants, Care Logs, photos and settings, and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes, whether Mail, Files, AirDrop or anywhere else, is your choice, and that service's own terms apply there.

Links

A Fun fact's source opens in your browser, under that website's own policy.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all data removes every plant, Care Log, photo and setting from the app, and deleting the app removes everything. Since the app sends us nothing, there's nothing for us to delete; write to us if you'd like that confirmed.

Contact

Green Friends is made by Guillem Arias, who is responsible for this policy (its controller under the GDPR). Write to greenfriends@gariasf.com with any question. An email you send is used only to answer it, and deleted once the conversation is over.

Changes

If the app is ever to send anything off your iPhone, this page will say so before the version that does is released.
