Support

Write to greenfriends@gariasf.com. If something isn't working, say which iPhone and which iOS version it's on.

Keeping your Garden

Settings › Export garden saves your whole Garden, with its plants, Care Logs, photos and settings, as one file. Settings › Import garden merges an Export back in: for each plant, Care Event and photo, the newer version wins, and nothing is wiped.

A new iPhone

Restore your iPhone backup, from iCloud or a computer, and your Garden comes with it. You can also Export on the old iPhone and Import on the new one.

The Growing season

Watering and fertilizing follow the Season: Growing from March to October and Dormant the rest of the year, unless you change it in Settings › Growing season. The same month for both keeps every plant Growing all year.

The Daily Digest

One notification a day, at the time set in Settings, and only on days a plant needs you. If none arrive, check Settings › Notifications › Green Friends on your iPhone.

Pets

A plant's pet toxicity comes from published references, such as the ASPCA's plant lists; where no reference settles it, the app says nothing. It's no substitute for a vet: if a pet has eaten a plant, call your vet or an animal poison line.
